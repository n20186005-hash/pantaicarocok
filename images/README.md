# Image provenance

The untraceable TripAdvisor JPG files were removed on 21 October 2026. Their author, exact original page and licence could not be established. They must not be restored without verifiable permission.

`public/images/coastal-bay-illustration.svg` and `coastal-shore-illustration.svg` are original project vector illustrations authored directly in SVG on 21 October 2026. They contain no external image material and depict imaginary coastal forms, not photography or geographical evidence of Pantai Carocok. Visible bilingual captions disclose this scope.

The existing `03-strait-chart-texture.png` and `04-coastal-field-notes.png` remain project-generated supporting illustrations; the favicon remains the existing project SVG mark.

`coastal-bay-social.png` is a 1200×630 raster rendering of the original bay SVG for Open Graph compatibility.

A repository-wide tracked-image review also removed unused `assets/pantai-carocok-sunset.jpeg`, `images/01-pantai-carocok-bay.jpg`, `images/02-pantai-carocok-shore.jpg`, `images/06-pantai-carocok.jpg` and `images/07-pantai-carocok.jpg` on 21 October 2026. No traceable source/licence record was available; unused copies are not exempt from provenance review. Remaining `images/03-*` and `images/04-*` duplicate the project-generated supporting illustrations; `images/05-*`, the two unused `assets/pantai-carocok-tide-mark*.png` brand studies and `public/favicon.svg` are project brand artwork, not destination photographs. The `assets/*chart-texture.png` and `assets/*coastal-notes.png` copies correspond to the same supporting illustrations.
